Transactions

Category edits apply to one transaction by default. For a friend's repayment, choose the original expense category to reduce your personal spending.

Merchant rules keep money in and money out separate. Individual transaction choices take priority. Changes are saved on this device.

How the numbers work

Money in counts salary, interest, refunds and other credits. Transfers between your own accounts are left out of every total.

Spent is everything else that left the account, except investments. Kept is money in minus spent, so it includes what you invested. Savings rate is kept as a share of money in.

Categories come from keywords in each description (Swiggy, BESCOM, Zerodha and so on). Anything unmatched lands in Other; fix one transaction or save a merchant rule in Transactions. Repayments assigned to a spending category reduce that category in the month received.

Privacy

This page has no server. Files are read with your browser's file reader and analysed in memory. Statements are stored only if you tick Remember on this device. Category choices are saved locally; transaction choices include the date, description, amount and balance. Clear data removes both statements and category choices.

Sample data is synthetic and does not describe a real person or account.
