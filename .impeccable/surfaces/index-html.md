# Surface: wdmmg dashboard (index.html)

Mode: Operate. Single local page; the owner loads Indian bank statement exports (CSV/XLS/XLSX) and reviews where money went, month on month and year on year. Sample data (synthetic, labelled) loads until a file is imported.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A modern finance dashboard that answers "what changed?" in words and comparisons first; it refuses the four-KPI-card + donut template.

OWN-WORLD: Cool paper-white ground, graphite ink, hairline rules instead of boxed cards, one jade accent for the selected month and saved money, persimmon for overspend, slate-graphite ramps for spending intensity; Anek Latin (Ek Type, native ₹) with tabular figures throughout; hand-drawn SVG charts on a shared ₹ scale.

STORY: The owner sees the month's verdict sentence, checks what moved versus last month and last year, traces where money flowed, then fixes categories and spots recurring leaks.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar (wordmark, on-device badge, import). A 24-month cash-flow chart doubling as the month selector; beside it the verdict sentence and a This/Last/Δ/Last-year/Δ comparison matrix.

FORM: Category canon (user-chosen after three re-rolls), executed at premium craft. Seed key f5f945f4.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
